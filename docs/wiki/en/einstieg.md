# Overview

**A fair sleep quota for multiplayer servers:** players who cannot or do not want to sleep no longer hold up the night, and with `/nosleep` you keep the night when you need it for a farm.

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 26.3 (or a fork like Purpur), Java 25 |
| Requires | nothing - LuckPerms is recommended, MRadmin optional |
| Languages | German, English - every player automatically in the language of their Minecraft client |
| Status | stable (1.x) |

## What can MRsleep do?
- **Sleep quota with exemptions:** bots, cam accounts, spectators and AFK players are not counted. The night is skipped as soon as enough *real* players sleep. → [Sleep quota with exemptions](schlafquote.md)
- **`/nosleep`:** a player blocks sleeping for one or more nights - perfect for mob farms or building projects at night. → [/nosleep](nosleep.md)
- **Admin menu:** all players as heads with live status. Bans, lifting the cooldown or releasing blocks with one click. → [Admin menu](admin-menue.md)
- **Display of your choice:** progress as boss bar, action bar or title, plus chat messages like "Alex went to bed (1/2)". → [Display & chat messages](anzeige.md)
- **Everything configurable:** one `config.yml`, all messages in language files, its own permission for every feature. → [Configuration](konfiguration.md)
- **Discord logs** via a webhook and an **update check** that informs you in game. → [Discord & log](discord.md)
